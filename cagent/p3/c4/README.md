# P3 C4: selected finite sizes

[Cell statement](https://hackathon.bainsa.ai/p/p3/c4) · [Package and replay instructions](../README.md)

Theorems `maximumDepth_11`, `maximumDepth_16`, and `maximumDepth_22` prove exact maximum first-periodic depths 8, 15, and 24, respectively, over **all** partitions of each size. These are finite instances, not a general C4 theorem. C4 remains partial.
